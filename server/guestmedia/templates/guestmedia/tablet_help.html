How the pulpit tablet works

A tablet on the pulpit shows your set. You tap a picture, video or song and it appears on the screen. Two minutes of reading, and nothing will surprise you on the day.

Your name Changing pages Is it live? Is vMix connected? Showing a photo Videos and songs Restart or resume Markers (cue points)

1. Your name at the top

The name in the top-left corner is the set the tablet is showing. The production team sends your set to the pulpit before you speak.

If you see someone else's name, don't tap anything: give a sign to the production desk and they will switch it to yours.

2. Changing pages

The tablet shows six files at a time, numbered in the order of your set. If you have more than six, use the arrows in the bottom-left corner; 1 / 2 means page one of two.

Changing pages never changes what is on the screen. Files picked from someone else's uploads show a small from … label.

3. Is it live?

The top-right corner always tells you what the room sees:

  • Screen shows the banner: nothing of yours is on screen.
  • Sending to the screen…: wait a moment (videos can take a few seconds).
  • A picture / A video is on screen or Audio is playing: it's live.

The tile that is live has a red frame and an ON SCREEN label.

4. Is vMix connected?

vMix is the program that puts your files on the screen. When the team turns the indicator on, you see a small vMix pill next to the status: a green dot means everything works, vMix offline with a red dot means taps won't reach the screen. Tell the desk.

If the tablet shows Reconnecting to the vMix PC…, it lost the Wi-Fi for a moment. The desk can always show your files from their own tablet, so just carry on speaking.

5. When you tap a photo

  1. Tap once. The tile turns orange: SENDING…
  2. A moment later it turns red: ON SCREEN. The room sees it.
  3. Tap the same photo again, or Clear screen, to go back to the banner. Tapping a different photo replaces it directly.

Tap once and wait for the colour: a second tap, even while it says SENDING…, takes it off again. A tile that says Downloading… is still being copied to the vMix PC and can't be shown yet.

6. Videos and songs

Video tiles show their length (Video · 0:24), songs say Audio. Tap to start. While it plays, a bar appears above the page arrows:

  • Pause / Play: the picture stays on screen while paused.
  • −10s / +10s: skip back or forward.
  • The time on the right counts down (0:16 left). At the end, the screen normally goes back to the banner by itself.
  • Songs usually play with the banner staying on screen; the tile says PLAYING and tapping it again stops the sound.

7. Restart a video or play from the start

  • From start in the playback bar jumps back to 0:00 while it is on screen.
  • If you stopped a video halfway (tap again or Clear screen), its tile shows Resume 1:23: tap that to continue where you left off. Tapping the picture itself starts again from the beginning.

8. Markers: start exactly where you want

A marker is a named point in a video or song, like Baptism · 1:12. Prepare them before the service so you can jump there live.

Add markers now, on this page

  1. Go to My set and tap Options under a video or song.
  2. Play it to the moment you want, then tap Add marker here, or type a name and a time (e.g. Baptism, 1:12) and tap Add.

Use them on the tablet

  • While the video plays, the marker buttons sit in the playback bar (Village · 0:10 in the picture above): tap one to jump there.
  • To start at a marker, tap the … button on the tile first. The Cue video window opens: the room doesn't see anything yet. Pick a marker (or slide along the pictures), then tap Start here.
  • In the cue window and in the playback bar, Marker / Add marker here saves the current point for next time.

If something goes wrong

Clear screen always brings the banner back. The production desk sees the same thing you see and can take over at any moment, so you can't break anything.
